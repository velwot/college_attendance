Google Drive backup

On the Dashboard, use the one Connect Google Drive button in the top corner. Once connected, attendance changes are automatically backed up to the private Google Drive app-data folder.

This app asks only for the restricted drive.appdata permission. It does not request access to your normal Google Drive files or your Gmail profile.

Safe restore checklist

Export before importing a different backup. An import replaces the local attendance record, then the Dashboard refreshes the subject totals from the restored data.
